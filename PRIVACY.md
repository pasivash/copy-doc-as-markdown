# Privacy Policy — Copy Doc as Markdown

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Copy Doc as Markdown does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

- **Document content.** When you click Copy or Download, the extension asks Google Docs for a .docx export of the document you have open (using your existing Google session) and reads the comment threads already loaded in the page. Everything is converted to Markdown locally in your browser. The result goes only to your clipboard or to a file you save.
- **No servers.** The extension makes no network requests other than to `docs.google.com` and the Google hosts that serve the export (`*.googleusercontent.com`). There is no analytics, tracking or remote code.
- **Settings.** Your preferences (which parts to include, whether to show the on-page buttons) are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`, which Chrome may sync between your own signed-in browsers. They contain no document data.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/pasivash/copy-doc-as-markdown/issues.
